BreachBox

A hands-on security training range. You attack a deliberately broken staff portal, capture the flag each bug hides, then submit it here to score.

How it works

  1. Exploit a challenge on the target app (the vulnerable zone, on port 5001).
  2. Copy the flag string it reveals.
  3. Submit it on your dashboard here. A short write-up is required, and hints cost points.
